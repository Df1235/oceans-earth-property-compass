| Area type | Simple explanation |

|---|---|

| **Carpet area** | The net usable floor area inside the apartment. Under India’s RERA definition, it includes internal partition walls but excludes external walls, service shafts, exclusive balconies/verandahs, and exclusive open terraces. |

| **Built-up area** | Generally, carpet area plus walls and attached balcony areas. The exact calculation can vary. |

| **Super built-up area** | Generally, built-up area plus an allocated share of common spaces, such as lobbies and corridors. Ask the developer what is included. |

| **Balcony / terrace area** | Outdoor space attached exclusively to the apartment, shown separately from RERA carpet area. |

Yes—add a simple “Understand Property Areas” tool with a labelled apartment illustration. Customers tap each term to see what it means:

Area type	Simple explanation

Carpet area	The net usable floor area inside the apartment. Under India’s RERA definition, it includes internal partition walls but excludes external walls, service shafts, exclusive balconies/verandahs, and exclusive open terraces.

Built-up area	Generally, carpet area plus walls and attached balcony areas. The exact calculation can vary.

Super built-up area	Generally, built-up area plus an allocated share of common spaces, such as lobbies and corridors. Ask the developer what is included.

Balcony / terrace area	Outdoor space attached exclusively to the apartment, shown separately from RERA carpet area.

Make it visual: highlight the rooms, walls, balcony, and shared spaces in different colours as customers select each term.
